FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to create an account?

No. Pixelence AI creates an anonymous account for you automatically the first time you open the app. Your coins, purchases and library are linked to it. Because it isn’t tied to an email address, use “Restore purchases” if you switch devices.

How do coins work?

Each effect uses a number of coins, shown before you start. You get coins with in-app purchases or a monthly subscription. If a generation fails, its coins are returned to you automatically. Coins have no cash value and can’t be transferred.

Are my photos used to train AI?

No. Your photos and the options you choose are used only to create the images you request. We don’t use them to identify anyone and we don’t use them to train AI models. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I use photos of other people?

Only photos of yourself or of people who have given you permission. Photos of children are only allowed if they show you as a child or you are the child’s parent or guardian (for example, to restore an old family photo). See the acceptable-use rules in our Terms of Use.

Can I use the passport photo for an official application?

It gives you a passport-style photo in a standard size on a white background. Many authorities don’t accept photos that have been edited or generated with AI, so check the official requirements for your country before you use it.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings (Settings › your name › Subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings › Account › Delete Account in the app. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it: coins, generation history, uploaded photos and generated images. No longer have the app? Email pixelence.app@gmail.com and we’ll delete your data within 30 days.
